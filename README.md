One single-line one-star review, at most 160 characters, of an everyday essential, for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xb3e689-one-star-review-of-something-essential-c7234629) bounty "One-Star Review of Something Essential".

## The review

> Sleep takes a third of my life, charges no rent, and still sends me back tired.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` — 79 characters, one line, no trailing newline. A gate validation runs first: the entry must be 1–160 characters, a single line with no surrounding whitespace, or the program fails fast instead of writing a bad line.
